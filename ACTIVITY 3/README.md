# 🦟 Activity: Dengue Awareness Infographic & Project Proposal
> *Promoting dengue prevention through information and community-based technology*

---

### ୨୧ About the Activity
This activity focused on creating an infographic about **Dengue: A Growing Threat** and preparing a project proposal for **DengueWatch**, a digital dengue prevention and community-based monitoring system. The infographic presents important information about dengue, its effects, common mosquito-breeding places, and people at risk. The project proposal introduces a digital platform where residents can report suspected breeding sites to help community health workers monitor and follow up on reports.

### ୨୧ What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I gained the following knowledge and skills:

- **Health Awareness & Communication:** Learned how to present important information about dengue, its prevention, and its effects on individuals and communities.
- **Visual Design & Organization:** Gained experience arranging facts, illustrations, and explanations into an informative and understandable infographic.
- **Community-Based Problem-Solving:** Understood how technology, such as DengueWatch, can help residents report mosquito-breeding sites and support community monitoring.
- **Problem-Solving:** One challenge was organizing the information without making the infographic and project proposal confusing. I addressed this by grouping related ideas and highlighting the most important details.
- **Future Application:** I can apply these skills in future health education activities and community projects while improving my ability to develop practical solutions to health concerns.

---

### ୨୧ Key Takeaway
This activity taught me that effective health education combines clear communication, visual presentation, and community participation. Technology can also support prevention by helping people work together to address health concerns.

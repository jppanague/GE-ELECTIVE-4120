# 📄 Activity 7: Resume Generating
> *Creating a Professional Nursing Resume Using AI Tools*

---

### ✦ About the Activity
This activity focused on generating a professional one-page resume using two AI tools, **ChatGPT and Gemini**. The goal was to create a clean, modern, and professional resume suitable for an entry-level nursing position or hospital On-the-Job Training (OJT) application. Both AI tools were given the same instructions, and their outputs were compared based on structure, visual presentation, formatting, and overall quality to determine which generated the better resume.

### ✦ What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I gained the following knowledge and skills:

- **Resume Writing:** Learned how to organize personal information, educational background, clinical skills, professional competencies, and certifications into a professional resume.
- **AI Tool Comparison:** Gained experience comparing ChatGPT and Gemini based on their ability to follow instructions and generate quality outputs.
- **Professional Presentation:** Understood the importance of proper formatting, visual hierarchy, readable typography, and organized sections in creating an effective resume.
- **Problem-Solving:** One challenge was choosing which AI-generated resume was more suitable for a professional nursing application. I addressed this by comparing both outputs based on their structure, readability, and overall appearance. Gemini produced the more visually appealing and organized result.
- **Future Application:** I can apply these skills when creating or updating my own professional resume and use AI tools more effectively while carefully reviewing their outputs.

---

### ✦ Key Takeaway
This activity taught me that AI tools can make resume creation easier, but comparing and evaluating their outputs is important to achieve a professional result. Choosing the right format and presenting relevant qualifications clearly can make a resume more effective.

### ୨୧ Activity Overview

This activity focused on developing a visual brand identity for a fictional milk tea business called **JAS Milk Tea Shop**. It involved creating a brand presentation that showcased the shop's logo, product inspirations, color palette, typography, and business details. The design used soft pastel colors, particularly lavender, pink, and baby blue, to create a refreshing, playful, and welcoming brand aesthetic.

### ୨୧ What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I gained a better understanding of the importance of branding and visual consistency.

- **Brand Identity:** Learned how to develop a brand concept by creating a logo, choosing a suitable business name, and establishing a consistent visual theme.
- **Color Theory:** Explored how different colors complement one another and how a pastel color palette can communicate a specific mood and personality.
- **Typography:** Learned how to select and combine fonts that match the brand's overall appearance while keeping the information readable.
- **Challenges & Solutions:** One challenge was choosing colors and design elements that would work well together. I addressed this by using a consistent pastel palette and organizing the different sections to maintain visual balance.
- **Future Application:** I can apply these skills when developing other brand identities, promotional materials, and digital designs. I also aim to improve my knowledge of color combinations, typography, and layout techniques.

### ୨୧ Key Takeaway

This activity taught me that building a brand is more than just creating a logo. It involves combining colors, typography, and visual elements to establish an identity that people can easily recognize and remember.
